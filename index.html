Getting to Mountain View & San Jose

For our loved ones traveling from afar, here is everything you need to know for a seamless trip to the Bay Area.

✈️

Airports

  • SJC (San Jose Mineta Airport): ~15 mins to Mountain View / central South Bay venues (Most convenient!).
  • SFO (San Francisco Airport): ~30-40 mins drive north.
  • OAK (Oakland Airport): ~40-45 mins drive across the bay.
🏨

Accommodations

We recommend hotels around Mountain View / Palo Alto, Downtown San Jose, or Santana Row / Valley Fair. Hotel block details will be shared in our formal invitations!

🚗

Getting Around

Rideshares (Uber/Lyft) and Caltrain/VTA transit are widely available throughout Mountain View and the South Bay. Car rentals are available at all major airports.

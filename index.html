Guest Logistics

Getting to San Jose & South Bay

For our friends and family traveling from afar, here are the convenient entry points to the Silicon Valley & South Bay area.

✈️

Airports

  • SJC (San Jose Mineta Airport): ~10-15 mins to central venues (Most convenient!).
  • SFO (San Francisco Airport): ~45-50 mins drive north.
  • OAK (Oakland Airport): ~40-45 mins drive across the bay.
🏨

Accommodations

We recommend looking at hotels around Downtown San Jose, Santana Row / Valley Fair, or Sunnyvale/Santa Clara. Hotel block details will be emailed in our formal invitation!

🚗

Getting Around

Rideshares (Uber/Lyft) are widely available throughout San Jose and the South Bay. Car rentals are also available at all major airports.

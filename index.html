Getting to San Jose

For our loved ones traveling to join us, here is everything you need for a seamless trip to North Park, San Jose.

✈️

Airports

  • SJC (San Jose Mineta Airport): ~10-15 mins drive to North Park and North San Jose hotels (Most convenient!).
  • SFO (San Francisco Airport): ~35-45 mins drive north via US-101.
  • OAK (Oakland Airport): ~40-50 mins drive across the bay via I-880.
🏨

Accommodations

We recommend hotels around North San Jose / River Oaks (closest to North Park), Downtown San Jose, or Santana Row / Valley Fair.

🚗

Getting Around

Rideshares (Uber/Lyft) and VTA light rail are widely available throughout San Jose and the North Park neighborhood. Car rentals are available at all Bay Area airports.
